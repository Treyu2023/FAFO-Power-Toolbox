          How a cloak route is chosen

          Wolven-cloak loads crowdsourced ALPR pins from OpenStreetMap for the corridor, then asks a public driving router for the direct route and a few alternates.

          If those pass inside your buffer, it tries a side street about two buffers off the pin and keeps the drive with the fewest pins. A detour more than about twice as long is dropped.

          Flock cameras along the line get a gold arrow and a wedge. The arrow is the mapped facing, clockwise from north. A camera with no direction tag is a gold dot. Other plate readers stay slate.

          T pins are turns. M pins are merges. Drag one to move that corner. The other pins stay, and the two legs next to the pin are the only part that changes. Flock arrows redraw on the new line.

          Volunteers miss cameras. An empty stretch is not a clear road.
