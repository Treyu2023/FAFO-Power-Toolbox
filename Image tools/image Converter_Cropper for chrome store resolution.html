Image Processor for Promo Tiles

Select an image file and choose the desired output size. Two modes are available:

  • Cover (Crop): Scales the image to cover the entire canvas, centrally cropping excess portions.
  • Contain (Letterbox): Scales the image to fit within the canvas while preserving the full image, adding solid black bars (OLED-friendly) on the sides or top/bottom as needed.

Export as JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no transparency.
