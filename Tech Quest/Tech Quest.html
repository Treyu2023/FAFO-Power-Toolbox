How to play

  • Class sets your kit; recruit along the route (up to 5 deploy)
  • Shops between tickets — equip weapons/armor (class restrictions; Leads wear anything)
  • Battle: click unit → blue move → Attack / skills / Wait → End turn
  • Rolls (d20): Field Tech −2 when On-Call (every 4th battle); Per Diem +2 party
  • Valkyrie Jump leaves the board, then Javelin of PTO rains down
  • Because I Said So scrambles enemy aggro · Shadowstep warps tiles
  • Boss tips: Quick E <10% → One More Ting; Methen → Reach for the Ceiling; Gilbest → Unbootstrap
