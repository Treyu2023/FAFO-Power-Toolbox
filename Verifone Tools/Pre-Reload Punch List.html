Pre-Reload Punch List

Creates a working copy of the master Pre-Reload Punch List spreadsheet and opens it. The master template under VerifoneLibrary\Templates is never modified.

  • Preferred path: site punchlists\ under your local Verifone sites root
  • Fallback: VerifoneLibrary\Working-PunchLists\ (gitignored)
  • For full site dossiers + prefill from SMS/XML, use Commander Site Console
▶ Launch punch list ⚙ Setup sites folder ⛽ Commander Console

PowerShell: VerifoneLibrary\Launch-PreReload-PunchList.ps1 · Optional args: Customer, SiteName
