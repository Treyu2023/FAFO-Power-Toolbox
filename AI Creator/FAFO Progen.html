How FAFO Progen works

  1. Assets (left) — your phrase pools. Drag a category or table into the workflow.
  2. Workflow (center) — staged sections. Set Weight (W) per stage, link categories, then Auto or drag blocks. ~N shows your phrase allocation.
  3. Output (right) — set Budget + Skip duplicates, watch Live preview, then Execute (Ctrl+Enter). Copy the result.

Skip duplicates (Output panel) — Auto/Reroll picks unique phrases; Execute drops repeats. Use workflow Dedupe to clean blocks already in the canvas.

Budget + Weight — set a global phrase Budget, then give each stage a Weight (W). A stage with weight 4 gets twice as many phrases as weight 2. The ~N badge shows the split before you Auto-fill.

Hold & reroll — 🔒 Hold stages you love (Auto/Reroll skip them). 🎲 on a stage rerolls only that stage. Toolbar Reroll free reshuffles everything not held. Pin 📌 individual phrases inside a free stage to keep them while the rest of that stage rerolls. Stage map: Alt+click hold · Shift+click reroll stage.

Live Ditto group (Output panel) — toggle Use Ditto group, pick OG NN / Paintfu / squirrel / etc. Execute and the Ditto Reel chip then fill from that live group (toolbox at 127.0.0.87). Nested includes sub-groups. Off = baked harvest.
